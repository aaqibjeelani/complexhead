About the complex

A cluster of schools, one shared standard.

The District Institute of Education and Training (DIET) at BHSS Hazratbal, Complex 121, brings together government and private schools from the Hazratbal zone of Srinagar. Each school maintains its own Class 8th Examination 2026 marks in a Google Sheet; this portal brings those sheets together so staff can enter marks and the read-only view helps verify the record. Official report cards are issued by DIET.

Government Boys Higher Secondary School (BHSS) Hazratbal is a public higher secondary school near the Kashmir University campus. Located on NIT Entrance Road, the school serves students of Classes 9 to 12 with English as the medium of instruction and functions under the Department of School Education, Jammu & Kashmir.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about entering marks and checking the Class 8th record.

Go to the school directory
How do I check the marks entered for a student?

Choose the school from the directory, then search its read-only sheet by roll number, student name, father’s name, or admission number. This view is for checking the entries in the school’s Google Sheet; it is not a report card.

My school’s sheet shows no data. What now?

The school has not entered its student marks in the linked Google Sheet yet. School staff can open the sheet from the school card and add the Class 8th marks. Once saved, the entries appear here after a refresh.

How does a school enter or update marks?

Find your school in the directory and choose Enter marks. The school’s editable Google Sheet opens in a new tab. Fill in the existing columns without renaming or rearranging them, then return to the read-only sheet view and refresh.

Are these the official marks?

The marks shown are read directly from the school-wise Google Sheets maintained by each school. The website does not edit, publish, or store the data. Official report cards are issued by DIET. For any correction or discrepancy, please contact the school office — the school record shall prevail.

Does this portal issue report cards?

No. Report cards are issued by DIET. Complexhead is only for schools to enter and verify marks in their Google Sheets. The website does not generate or print individual report cards.

Can I print the result sheet?

Yes. The read-only school sheet can be printed for internal reference using Print sheet on the school page. This prints the tabular marks sheet, not an individual report card.

I can’t find my name by roll number.

Try searching by your name, father’s name, or admission number instead. If your record still does not appear, it may not have been entered yet — please contact your school office.

Which schools are part of Complex 121?

The complex includes GHS Hazratbal, BMS Hazratbal, BMS Bagat-i-Shor, Govt Middle School Batapora, Greenland High School Nigeen, Legends School of Education Sadrabal, New Lifeline Educational Institute, Crescent Public School, RP School Malbagh, University Model School, and Vision School of Education. All 11 are listed in the school directory above.

Need help?

We’re here to help.

For help with your school’s Google Sheet, missing marks, or corrections to the Class 8th examination data, contact the school office or the complex head at BHSS Hazratbal. Report cards are issued by DIET.
